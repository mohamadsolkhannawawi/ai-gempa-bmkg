FDSN: Data Center: GEOSHAKE International Federation of Digital Seismograph Networks About FDSN Data Centers Mailing Lists Meetings Membership Networks Publications Services Structure Terms of Reference Working Groups Contact Us Sign in Home Data Centers GEOSHAKE Data Center: GEOSHAKE Name GEOSHAKE Full Name GeoShake Data Center Summary GeoShake operates the data services for the GW network, a citizen seismic network of low-cost MEMS accelerometer stations hosted by private individuals. All data is served anonymously under CC BY 4.0: no registration, no API key and no rate agreement. A public limit of 100 requests per 5 minutes per IP applies purely as abuse protection and is not an access restriction. Two standard services are live: fdsnws-station (StationXML 1.1, including level=response with a nominal instrument sensitivity) and fdsnws-event (QuakeML 1.2). Real-time continuous and triggered waveforms are available over SeedLink at seedlink.geoshake.org:18000, and individual triggers can be downloaded as SAC from the station pages. fdsnws-dataselect is not implemented yet, so this data centre cannot currently serve archived waveform windows through the standard FDSN route. We would rather leave the field empty than advertise an endpoint that returns 404.
