[2026-09-24T09:57:59.302Z] pevans: After discussions with BMKG, GEOFON has regained real-time access within GFZ to streams for the GEOFON-BMKG cooperated stations in Indonesia. We are now able to use these in the earthquake locations we provide again. However we are unable to redistribute these waveforms to others. We regret the inconvenience, and loss of reproducibility, that the seismic community experiences as a result of these decisions. As before, if you require access to real time data from these stations, please contact BMKG and make your need known to them. We still hope to be able to redistribute these again in future, but we have no planned date for this yet, nor do we know whether we will be allowed to do so.
